  2) HIGH-PRESSURE PUMP CALCULATION

  Seawater RO pressure: ~60 bar (6,000 kPa)

  Pump power equation: P = (Q × ΔP) / (η × 1000)

  Where: Q = m³/sec ΔP = Pa η = pump efficiency (assume 0.8)

  Q = 18.52 m³/hr = 0.00514 m³/sec ΔP = 6,000,000 Pa

  P = (0.00514 × 6,000,000) / (0.8 × 1000) P = 30,840 / 800 P
  ≈ 38.6 kW

  High pressure pump ≈ 40 kW motor

  Energy Recovery Device (ERD): Typical efficiency ~50%

  Actual electrical draw ≈ 20–25 kW continuous

  Daily energy: 25 kW × 24 hr = 600 kWh/day

  Energy per m³: 600 kWh ÷ 200 m³ = 3 kWh/m³
  ------------------------------------------------------------

3)  MEMBRANE COUNT CALCULATION

Standard seawater membrane: ~6.5 m³/day per element (8” SWRO)

Needed: 200 ÷ 6.5 ≈ 31 elements

Round to: 32 membranes

Configuration: 4 pressure vessels 8 elements per vessel

Total: 4 × 8 = 32 elements

Operating flux: ~15–18 LMH

6)  COMPUTE COOLING CALCULATION

Compute load: 40 kW thermal

Cooling water ΔT = 10°C

ṁ = Q / (cp × ΔT)

Q = 40,000 W cp = 4186 J/kg°C ΔT = 10°C

ṁ = 40,000 / (4186 × 10) = 0.955 kg/sec

≈ 3.4 m³/hr coolant flow

Heat exchanger sizing:

Q = U A ΔT

Assume U = 1200 W/m²K ΔT log mean ≈ 8°C

A = Q / (U × ΔT) = 40,000 / (1200 × 8) = 40,000 / 9600 ≈ 4.17 m²

Plate exchanger ~5 m² area sufficient.
